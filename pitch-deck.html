The Problem

Bangladesh's shops still run on paper ledgers
— and it's costing them.

Manual khata

Sales, dues and EMI tracked in notebooks. Reconciliation takes days each month; errors and theft go unnoticed.

Serialized inventory

Mobile & electronics shops must track every IMEI/serial, warranty and repair job — generic khata apps can't do this.

Unreliable internet

Cloud-only POS tools stop when connectivity drops. Shops need to sell offline and sync later.

Source: field interviews (in progress), BBS Wholesale & Retail Trade Survey
The Solution

A complete business OS for retail shops.

  • POS, purchase, returns, stock transfer — full inventory lifecycle
  • IMEI/serial tracking, warranty, exchange — built for phone & electronics retail
  • Batch & expiry tracking with FEFO — the pharmacy counter's core need
  • EMI / installment sales & credit limits — how BD customers actually buy
  • Repair job orders & servicing — the mobile shop's second business
  • HRM, CRM, expenses, khata reports — one system, not five apps
  • AI purchase entry & WhatsApp/SMS gateway — modern differentiators

Offline-first sync

Sell with zero connectivity; local DB syncs to the cloud when back online. Built for real infrastructure conditions.

True multi-tenant SaaS

Schema-per-tenant isolation, AES-256-GCM encrypted PII, subscription plans — built to scale to thousands of shops.

Every device they own

Android app, Windows desktop installer, web app — plus a ready-made storefront website per shop.

All features above are implemented and in production builds
