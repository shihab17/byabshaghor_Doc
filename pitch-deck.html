Beachhead #2 · Pharmacy

226K+ pharmacies lose stock to expiry every year.

The pain

Medicines expire silently on shelves. Owners find out when the box is already dead stock — written off, or worse, sold by mistake. Batch recall? Nearly impossible on paper.

The units mess

Stock arrives in boxes, sells by strip, sometimes by piece. Keeping the math straight across a thousand SKUs is where errors and theft hide.

  • Batch + expiry tracked at purchase — every unit knows its shelf life
  • FEFO allocation at billing — oldest expiry sells first, automatically
  • Expiry alert report — see what's dying 30/60/90 days out, return or discount it
  • Strip ↔ piece unit conversion — sell either way, stock stays correct
  • Offline-first + multi-device — counter keeps selling, syncs later

2,26,118

registered pharmacies in Bangladesh (DGDA) + ~1 lakh unlicensed — the densest retail network in the country
