Beachhead #2 · Pharmacy
বাংলা ↗ 07 / 11
Beachhead #2 · Pharmacy Niche

226K+ pharmacies lose stock to expiry every single year.

⚠️ Silent Expiry Loss

Medicines expire silently on shelves. Owners discover dead stock when it's too late — forced to write it off, or worse, sell it by error. Paper batch recall is virtually impossible.

📦 The Multi-Unit Dilemma

Stock arrives in whole boxes, sells by strip, sometimes by piece. Keeping math straight across thousands of SKUs is where inventory errors and employee theft hide.

  • Batch & expiry tracked at purchase — every unit knows its exact shelf life
  • Automatic FEFO billing — first-expiry-first-out sells older stock automatically
  • Expiry alert reports — see what expires in 30/60/90 days; return or discount
  • Box ↔ Strip ↔ Piece unit conversion — sell any unit, inventory stays synced
  • Full offline counter — load-shedding or net drop will never halt a billing line
2,26,118+
Registered pharmacies (DGDA) + ~1 lakh community drugstores — Bangladesh's densest retail network.
